Capture the Sky

An outdoor workshop where students look up at the sky and take their own sky photos.

The Challenge

Students spend a lot of time indoors and don't often look at the sky.

Our Solution

We went to Riverwood Park for a sky walk and learned some easy phone photo tips.

Results

  • 18 students joined
  • Over 120 sky photos taken
