WORKSHOP FEATURE

Capture the Sky

An outdoor workshop where students look up at the sky and take their own sky photos.

The Challenge

Students spend a lot of time indoors and don't often look at the sky. Many also think they need a fancy camera to take good photos.

Our Solution

We went to Riverwood Park for a sky walk. Students learned easy phone photo tips about light and framing, then shared their favourite shot with the group.

Results
